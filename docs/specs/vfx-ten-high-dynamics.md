Adds ten high-dynamics procedural effects to the production VFX pipeline: Nova, Vortex, Meteor, Rift, Arc Chain, Gravity Well, Blade Dance, Seismic Wave, Soul Reap and Time Warp. Each has a distinct closed-form motion composition sampled from normalized TTL progress, is registered for Canvas and Skia through the shared IDrawAdapter, and appears in Visual Lab with four reproducible temporal presets (contact, expansion, peak, dissipation). No frame clock, random source, scheduler or secondary particle runtime is introduced. Tests cover deterministic same-seed output, changed geometry across TTL samples and registration parity. CI and visual review remain acceptance gates; adapter tests do not prove pixel parity or final visual quality.
